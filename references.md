## Related Work

### Themes

- Systems theory on weakest-link survival and network persistence.

### The endurance criterion $R = d(\ln I)/d(\ln C)$

This quantity is an **elasticity** (log-log slope, scaling exponent), and is
formalized under other names across several disciplines. The correspondence is
mapped in detail in [`analysis/prior_art.md`](analysis/prior_art.md); the minimum
citation set is:

- **Tainter, J. (1988).** *The Collapse of Complex Societies.* Cambridge University Press.  
  Declining marginal returns on complexity as a collapse mechanism.
- **Ashby, W. R. (1956).** *An Introduction to Cybernetics.* Chapman & Hall.  
  The Law of Requisite Variety — sets the lower edge of the viability band.
- **Holling, C. S. & Gunderson, L. H. (2002).** *Panarchy: Understanding Transformations in Human and Natural Systems.* Island Press.  
  The rigidity trap and the poverty trap — both failure modes of a two-sided band.
- **Bettencourt, L. M. A. & West, G. B. (2007).** "Growth, innovation, scaling, and the pace of life in cities." *PNAS* 104(17), 7301–7306.  
  Urban scaling exponents; superlinear growth and finite-time singularities.
- **Rissanen, J. (1978).** "Modeling by shortest data description." *Automatica* 14(5), 465–471.  
  Minimum Description Length — the criterion $\Delta I \ge \Delta C$ in bits.
- **Van Valen, L. (1973).** "A New Evolutionary Law." *Evolutionary Theory* 1, 1–30.  
  The Red Queen — the coevolutionary floor.

Further correspondences (Kleiber's law, West–Brown–Enquist metabolic scaling, the
Information Bottleneck, neural scaling laws, loss-of-complexity theories of aging,
exploration/exploitation and competency traps) are catalogued in
[`analysis/prior_art.md`](analysis/prior_art.md).
